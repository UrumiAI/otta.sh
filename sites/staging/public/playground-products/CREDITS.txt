Photographs shipped with the Otta playground sample products.

These are Otta-branded commerce shots of the three sample products, in the
same lineup as the Otta marketing page (UrumiAI/otta-landing-page PR #9):
a black heavyweight tee, a cream speckled mug, and a sheet of die-cut
stickers, each with the OTTA wordmark, on a warm off-white studio ground.

The landing repository is private to this workspace, so these files are
not a byte copy of that repo's originals. They were produced to match the
product photographs in the landing page's marketing frames, and they
replace the earlier Unsplash photographs. They are static JPEGs in this
directory. The playground serves them with a plain <img> and the passthrough
image service. They do not use Cloudflare Images.
